Sortaire: Word Card Solitaire

Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 27, 2026

Liam Petersen ("we," "our," or "us") built Sortaire: Word Card Solitaire as a commercial app. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and disclosed when you use our app.

Information Collection

Sortaire does not require an account, login, or registration. We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The app does not collect or transmit any personal data directly. However, third-party services integrated into the app may collect certain information as described below.

Third-Party Services

The app uses the following third-party services that may collect information:

  • AppLovin MAX — advertising mediation platform used to display ads
  • RevenueCat — in-app purchase management service

These services operate under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • AppLovin Privacy Policy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

Advertising

Sortaire displays ads through AppLovin MAX. The ad network and its mediation partners may collect device identifiers (such as IDFA on iOS) and usage data to serve personalized advertisements.

The app uses Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. Before any tracking occurs, you will be asked for permission through a system prompt. If you decline, ads will still be shown but will not be personalized based on tracking data.

We do not use a separate analytics service. Any analytics data is collected solely through the ad SDK as part of its standard operation.

In-App Purchases

Sortaire offers in-app purchases processed through Apple's App Store and managed by RevenueCat. When you make a purchase, transaction data (such as purchase receipts and subscription status) may be collected and processed by RevenueCat to validate and manage your purchases.

We do not have access to your payment information. All payment processing is handled by Apple.

Data Storage

Your game progress, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device using AsyncStorage. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

Local data is automatically deleted when you uninstall the app. We do not maintain any backups or copies of your local game data.

Children's Privacy

Sortaire is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided information through a third-party service within the app, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
